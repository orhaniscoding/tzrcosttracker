# Code of conduct

[Türkçe](docs/tr/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in TZR Cost Tracker respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone. We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback focused on the work.
- Protect private data, credentials, and customer information.
- Use project channels for project work and follow the project's private process for reporting vulnerabilities. [Security policy](SECURITY.md)

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, and disruptive personal attacks are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. If you need a private channel, ask the maintainer for one through a project issue and leave sensitive details out of that issue. The maintainer will review reports and handle them as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
