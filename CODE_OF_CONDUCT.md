# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in TZR Cost Tracker respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone. We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback focused on the work.
- Protect private data, credentials, and customer information.
- Use project channels for project work and follow the security reporting process for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, and disruptive personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
